Resume, work badge and student ID photos: sizes and how to make them

Not every photo you need is a passport photo. A resume, a work badge or a student ID usually comes with its own size, set by the employer, school or application portal, and often as an upload rather than a print. Here is how to get the size right and make the photo on your iPhone with Snapport, which has templates for resume, work-badge and student ID photos plus fully custom sizes.

Take a good original

  • Face a window for soft, even light.
  • Stand in front of a plain wall.
  • Have someone else take it at eye level, and take a few shots.

Make it in Snapport

  1. Pick the resume, work-badge or student ID template, or create a custom size.
  2. Line up the face with on-device Apple Vision face detection and head-top, eye-line and chin guides. Pinch, drag and rotate, or auto-center.
  3. Background: replace it with white, gray, blue or red, or keep the original.
  4. Light touch-ups: identity-safe adjustments to brightness, contrast, warmth, exposure and sharpness. Keep it looking like you.
  5. Export as JPEG, PNG or PDF, or make a 4×6, A4 or Letter print sheet with cut guides.

Frequently asked questions

What size should a resume photo be?

It depends on the job posting, portal or local custom. Snapport includes a resume template, and if you are given exact dimensions you can create a custom size in mm, inches or pixels at any DPI.

Can I make a work badge photo at home?

Yes. Snapport has a work-badge template and custom sizes. Follow the size and background your employer asks for.

Can I make a student ID photo for an online upload?

Yes. Use the student ID template or a custom pixel size, then export as JPEG or PNG and save to Photos or Files.

Can I change the background?

Yes. Snapport can replace the background with white, gray, blue or red, or keep the original. Check what your employer or school asks for.
