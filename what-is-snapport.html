What is Snapport?

Snapport is an app for taking passport, visa and ID photos at home. It gives you common size templates (for example US 2×2, UK/Schengen/Japan/Korea/Australia 35×45 mm, Canada 50×70 mm, China 33×48 mm and India 2×2) plus custom sizes, on-device face detection with head-top, eye-line and chin guides, and print sheets for 4×6, A4 or Letter with cut guides. Snapport prepares the photo only; the document itself is issued by the relevant authority or organization.

Who is Snapport for?

People who need a passport, visa, ID card, student ID, resume or work-badge photo and would rather take it at home than go to a photo studio, and anyone who wants the photo processed privately on the phone. Photo requirements vary by country, agency and application type, and Snapport does not guarantee acceptance, so always check the official requirements before submitting.

How does Snapport charge?

Snapport is a paid app: you buy it once and it is yours. The listing states there is no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking and no account. On the US App Store it costs $5.99 (checked 2026-11-15); prices differ in other countries.

How is Snapport different from other passport photo apps?

Based on what the listing describes: everything is processed on the device and photos are never uploaded; it works fully offline, and the only network use is an optional, user-started template update check that downloads a public size list and sends no personal data; it is a single purchase with no subscription or in-app purchases; you can create custom sizes in mm, inches or pixels at any DPI. Snapport is not affiliated with any government.
