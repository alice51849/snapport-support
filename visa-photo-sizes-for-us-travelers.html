Visa photo sizes for US travelers

Your US passport photo is 2×2 inches, but many other countries ask for a different size for their visa applications. Showing up with the wrong size is an easy mistake to avoid. Below are common sizes that Snapport has templates for, and how to make each one from a single photo session at home. Each country sets its own rules and can change them, so always check the embassy or consulate’s latest official requirements before you submit.

Common visa photo sizes

Other details, such as head size, background color and how recent the photo must be, differ by country and visa type. Look them up on the official site of the country you are visiting.

One photo session, every size

Set up once: face a window for soft, even light, stand in front of a plain wall, and have someone else take a few shots with the rear camera at eye level. Then in Snapport, open the template for each destination and position the same photo. Head-top, eye-line and chin guides from on-device Apple Vision face detection help you line it up, and you can pinch, drag, rotate or auto-center.

Background colors and touch-ups

Snapport can replace the background with white, gray, blue or red, or keep the original, and offers light adjustments. Some documents do not allow digital changes at all, so check the official rules for each visa before you use these tools.

Export or print

Export each photo as JPEG, PNG or PDF for online applications, or create a 4×6, A4 or Letter print sheet with multiple copies and cut guides for paper forms.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my US passport photo for a Schengen visa?

Usually not as-is: the Schengen template is 35×45 mm, while a US passport photo is 2×2 inches. Make a separate photo at the right size, and check the consulate’s latest rules.

What size is a China visa photo?

Snapport includes a China 33×48 mm template. Check the official requirements for your application before submitting.

What if my destination is not in the list?

Snapport has templates for many regions, and you can create a custom size in mm, inches or pixels at any DPI and with any background.

Does the app work without internet while traveling?

Yes. Snapport works fully offline; the only network use is an optional template update check that you start yourself.

Who is Snapport for, and why is it a one-time purchase?

Snapport is for anyone who would rather make passport, visa and ID photos at home, privately, on their own iPhone. You buy it once and it is yours: no subscription, no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking, no account. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded.
