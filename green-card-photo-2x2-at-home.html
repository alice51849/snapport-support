The size: 2×2 inches

A 2×2 photo is a 2 by 2 inch square (51 × 51 mm). Snapport includes a US 2×2 template, so the crop is already the right shape and size. Everything else, such as how recent the photo must be, background and pose, is set by the official instructions for your application.

Take the photo

  1. Read the official instructions for your form first, so you know what background and pose they expect.
  2. Use soft, even light from the front, for example facing a window.
  3. Stand in front of a plain, light wall a little away from it, so your shadow does not fall on it.
  4. Have someone else take it at eye level, and take several shots.

Line up the 2×2 crop with Snapport

  1. Choose the US 2×2 template.
  2. Position the face. On-device Apple Vision face detection shows head-top, eye-line and chin guides. Pinch, drag and rotate; you can auto-center the face and reset anytime.
  3. Background: Snapport can replace the background with white, gray, blue or red, or keep the original. Many official applications do not accept edited photos, so check the instructions and keep the original when in doubt.
  4. Export as JPEG, PNG or PDF, and save to Photos or Files, or share.

Print it on a 4×6 sheet

Make a print sheet for 4×6, A4 or Letter with multiple copies and cut guides. Order it as a regular 4×6 print, turn off auto-enhance and auto-crop, and measure before cutting: each photo should be exactly 2 by 2 inches. More tips: Print 2x2 photos on a 4x6 sheet.
