Canadian passport photo at home: the 50×70 mm size

Canada uses a larger photo than many countries: 50×70 mm, compared with the 35×45 mm common in Europe or the US 2×2. Snapport has a Canada 50×70 mm template, so you can set up the photo at home, line it up on your iPhone and print it on a sheet with cut guides. Canadian passport photo rules are set by the Government of Canada and can include details about who takes and prints the photo, so read the latest official requirements before you start and before you submit.

Why the 50x70 mm size matters

A photo made for another country’s smaller format will not match the Canadian size. Choosing the right template from the start saves a reprint. Snapport includes Canada 50×70 mm alongside US 2×2, 35×45 mm, China 33×48 mm, India 2×2 and more.

Set up at home

  1. Face a window for soft, even light.
  2. Stand a little away from a plain, light-coloured wall so your shadow does not fall on it.
  3. Have someone else take the photo with the rear camera at eye level.
  4. Glasses off, hair away from the face, look straight at the lens.
  5. Take several shots and pick the sharpest one.

Line it up in Snapport

Choose the Canada 50×70 mm template. On-device Apple Vision face detection shows head-top, eye-line and chin guides; pinch, drag and rotate, or auto-center the face, and reset anytime. Export as JPEG, PNG or PDF.

Print it

Create a print sheet for 4×6, A4 or Letter with multiple copies and cut guides. Print at actual size with auto-crop turned off, measure one photo before cutting, and trim with a ruler and blade. If the official rules require a photo studio or specific print details, follow those rules.
