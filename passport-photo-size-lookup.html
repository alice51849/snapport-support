Common questions

How many pixels is a 2x2 passport photo?

2 × 2 inches is 600 × 600 pixels at 300 DPI and 1200 × 1200 pixels at 600 DPI. For an online US visa upload, the State Department asks for a square image between 600 × 600 and 1200 × 1200 pixels.

How many pixels is a 35x45 mm photo?

About 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI and 827 × 1063 pixels at 600 DPI (1 inch = 25.4 mm, rounded to the nearest pixel).

Is a 35x45 mm photo the same as 2x2 inches?

No. 2 × 2 inches is about 51 × 51 mm and square. 35 × 45 mm is narrower and taller. Trimming one into the other changes how big your head is in the frame, so start from the right size.

Does this tool upload my photo?

No. The lookup only shows sizes. It does not ask for a photo, and nothing you select is sent anywhere.

Where can I check the exact rules for my application?

With the office that receives your application, such as the U.S. Department of State for a US passport, IRCC for a Canadian passport, or the embassy or consulate for a visa. The numbers here are a starting point; that office has the final word.
