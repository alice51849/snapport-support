US passport photo requirements: a 2x2 checklist and how to take one at home

A US passport photo does not have to come from a photo counter, but it does have to meet the State Department’s rules, and photos are one of the most common reasons an application gets held up. Below is the checklist in plain English, how to set up light and background at home, how to line up the 2x2 crop on your iPhone, and how to print it. One rule shapes everything else: the photo must not be digitally altered, so the job of any app here is sizing and positioning, not editing. Always check the official page before you submit, because rules can change.

Set up at home: light, wall and camera

No app can rescue a bad original, and for a passport you are not allowed to edit your way out of one. Five minutes of setup is what makes the photo work.

  1. Face a window. Soft daylight from the front gives even light. Light from the side puts shadows under your eyes and nose; light from behind turns your face dark.
  2. Stand about 3 feet from a plain white or off-white wall. That distance keeps your shadow off the wall. If you do not have a light wall, tape a large sheet of white poster board behind you.
  3. Have someone else take it. Rear camera, held upright at eye level, about 4 feet away. A selfie at arm’s length distorts the face and is not accepted.
  4. Glasses off, hat off, hair away from the face. Look straight into the lens with both eyes open and a neutral expression or a natural smile.
  5. Take five shots in a row. Then choose the one where your head is straight and your eyes are fully open.

Print it: a 4x6 sheet with cut guides

For a paper application you need a printed photo. In Snapport, make a print sheet for 4×6, A4 or Letter with multiple copies and cut guides. A 4×6 sheet is handy because any drugstore or online photo service prints it as a standard photo.

  • Order it as a regular 4×6 print on glossy or matte photo paper.
  • Turn off auto-enhance, auto-crop and “fit to page” so the size does not change.
  • Before cutting, measure: the square should be exactly 2 by 2 inches.
  • Cut along the guides with a ruler and a sharp blade rather than scissors.

Frequently asked questions

Can I take my own US passport photo at home?

Yes, as long as it meets the State Department’s requirements. It should be taken by someone else rather than as a selfie. Snapport prepares the photo only; it does not guarantee acceptance and is not affiliated with any government.

What size is a US passport photo?

2 x 2 inches, with the head measuring 1 to 1 3/8 inches from chin to top of head. Snapport includes a US 2×2 template with head-top, eye-line and chin guides.

Can I change the background to white in an app?

Not for a US passport photo: digitally altered photos are not accepted. Take it against a real white or off-white wall and keep the original background.

Can I wear glasses?

No, unless you cannot remove them for medical reasons, in which case a signed statement from your doctor is needed.
