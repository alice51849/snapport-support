How to print 2x2 passport photos on a 4x6 sheet

A 4×6 print is the cheapest, easiest way to get a paper passport photo: almost every drugstore, photo kiosk and online print service makes them. The trick is putting your 2×2 photos on that sheet at the exact size, with lines that show you where to cut. This page walks through the layout, the print order and the trimming. Requirements for the photo itself vary by agency, so check the latest official rules before you submit.

Why a 4x6 sheet works for 2x2 photos

A 4×6 inch print is a standard photo size, so you order it like any vacation snapshot. Two-inch squares fit on it with room to spare, which means one print gives you several copies of the same photo: handy when an application asks for more than one, or when a cut goes wrong.

Make the sheet in Snapport

  1. Pick the US 2×2 template. Snapport includes popular sizes such as US 2×2, and you can create custom sizes in mm, inches or pixels at any DPI.
  2. Line up your face. On-device Apple Vision face detection shows head-top, eye-line and chin guides. Pinch, drag and rotate, or auto-center the face.
  3. Create a print sheet. Choose 4×6 (A4 and Letter are also available). Snapport places multiple copies and adds cut guides.
  4. Export as JPEG, PNG or PDF, then save to Photos or Files, or share it straight to a print service.

Ordering the print without resizing

  • Order a regular 4×6 print on photo paper.
  • Turn off auto-enhance, auto-crop and “fit to page”. Any of these can shrink or stretch the photo.
  • If you print at home, set the scale to 100% / actual size.
  • When the print arrives, measure one square with a ruler before cutting: it should be exactly 2 by 2 inches.

Cutting along the guides

Use the cut guides on the sheet, a metal ruler and a sharp craft blade on a cutting mat. Scissors tend to drift and leave uneven edges. Cut the long lines first, then the short ones, and keep your fingers off the face area of the photo.

Frequently asked questions

How many 2x2 photos fit on a 4x6 print?

Several copies fit on one 4×6 sheet. Snapport lays out multiple copies for you and adds cut guides between them.

Can I print the sheet at a drugstore or photo kiosk?

Yes. Export the sheet as JPEG or PDF and order it as a standard 4×6 print. Turn off auto-crop and auto-enhance so the size stays exact.

My printed photo came out the wrong size. What happened?

Usually the printer or service scaled the image to fit. Reprint at 100% / actual size with cropping turned off, and measure before you cut.
